Make a playlist of your TikTok sounds

TikTok’s Favorites keep sounds for making videos, not for listening. In Tikify your sounds go in playlists you can shuffle, repeat and play anywhere, even offline.

Make a playlist

  1. Create it

    In Your Library, tap New playlist and give it a name.

  2. Add sounds

    Open the playlist and tap + to pick sounds from your library, or add a new TikTok sound straight into it from the playlist’s ⋮ menu. When you save a new sound, you can tick the playlists it goes in.

  3. Play

    Tap the green play button, or turn on shuffle first to play in random order.

Every sound you save is also in All sounds, the playlist with your whole library.

What you can do with a playlist

  • Shuffle and repeat. Repeat the whole playlist, or loop one sound.
  • Play next. From a sound’s ⋯ menu, put it up next without changing the playlist.
  • Put sounds in order. Use Move up and Move down in a sound’s menu.
  • One sound, many playlists. A sound can be in as many playlists as you like: Add to playlist in its menu.
  • Tidy up. Edit a sound’s title and artist, rename or delete playlists, and remove a sound from one playlist without deleting it.
  • Search. Find any sound or playlist in your library by name.
  • Listen anywhere. Playlists play offline and with the screen locked.

Playlist ideas

  • Gym: the high-energy sounds from workout videos.
  • Sleep and calm: rain, lo-fi and ambient sounds on repeat.
  • Study: focus music, without the videos pulling you back into scrolling.
  • Trending: the songs everyone is using this month, before you forget them.
  • Funny: voice clips and memes to play for your friends.
  • Dance practice: the sounds behind the dances you’re learning, looped one at a time.

Take your playlists to a new phone

Your library is stored on your device, not in an account. To move it, open Your Library → ⋮ → Export backup on the old device and Import backup on the new one. The backup holds your sounds, covers and playlists.
