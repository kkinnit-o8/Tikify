Install Tikify on iPhone and Android

Tikify is a web app, so there’s nothing to download from the App Store or Google Play. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen, works offline and keeps playing in the background, like any other app.

On iPhone, install before you save sounds. The Home Screen app has its own storage, separate from Safari, so sounds you save in Safari won’t show up in it. Already saved some? Move them over with a backup.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open tikify.no in Safari.

  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow) in Safari’s toolbar. On iOS 26, tap ⋯ first, then Share.

  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. If you don’t see it, scroll down or tap View More.

  4. Tap Add, then open Tikify from your Home Screen.

In Chrome, Edge or Firefox on iPhone, tap Share in the address bar or the ⋯ menu, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open tikify.no in Chrome.

  2. Tap ⋮ at the top right to open the menu.

  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

Chrome may also offer to install Tikify by itself. Once it’s installed, you can share a TikTok straight to Tikify: Share → More → Tikify.

Opened Tikify from inside TikTok or Instagram?

Links opened inside TikTok, Instagram and similar apps load in their built-in browser, which can’t install web apps and may throw away what you save. Tap ⋯ or ⋮, choose Open in Safari, Open in Chrome or Open in browser, and install from there.

On a computer

  • Chrome or Edge: click the install icon in the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose Install Tikify.
  • Safari on Mac: choose File → Add to Dock.

Move your sounds into the installed app, or to a new phone

  1. Where your sounds are now (in Safari, say), open Your Library → ⋮ → Export backup and save the file.
  2. Open the installed Tikify, or Tikify on the new phone, and choose Your Library → ⋮ → Import backup. Pick the file.

Updates

Tikify updates itself. When a new version is ready you’ll see “A new version of Tikify is ready”: tap Reload, or it’s used from the next time you open Tikify.
