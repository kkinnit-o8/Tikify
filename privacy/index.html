Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026

The short version: Tikify has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. Your library stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your sounds: the audio, the cover, the title and artist, and the link of the TikTok they came from.
  • Your playlists.
  • A few settings, such as what was playing, shuffle and repeat, when you last made a backup, and whether you’ve dismissed the install tip.

They’re kept in your browser’s storage (IndexedDB and local storage) on this device only. Tikify can’t see them, and they aren’t synced anywhere.

What is sent to others

  • tikwm.com. When you look up a link, Tikify sends that link to tikwm.com, a free third-party service, to find the video’s sound. Like any website, tikwm.com also sees your IP address.
  • TikTok’s servers. Sounds and covers are downloaded, and previews played, from TikTok’s content servers. They see your IP address, as when you watch TikTok in a browser.
  • GitHub Pages. Tikify’s own files are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser loads them and may keep it in its logs for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Nothing else: Tikify has no ads, trackers, analytics or cookies.

Backups

A backup is a file you save yourself, with your whole library in it. It goes wherever you put it: Tikify doesn’t upload it.

Deleting your data

Delete sounds and playlists in the app. To remove everything, delete Tikify from your home screen and clear the website data for tikify.no in your browser’s settings.
