Frequently asked questions

Everything about saving TikTok sounds with Tikify and playing them like music.

Getting started

What is Tikify?

Tikify is a free web app that saves the sounds of TikTok videos and plays them like music. You paste a TikTok link, Tikify saves the sound to your device, and you play it in playlists, offline and with your phone locked.

Is Tikify free? Do I need an account?

It’s free, and there’s no account or sign-up. Open tikify.no and start saving sounds.

Is Tikify in the App Store or Google Play?

No. Tikify is a web app: you open it in your browser and can add it to your home screen, where it works like a regular app. See Install Tikify.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and computers, in a current version of Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Saving sounds

Which links work?

The link of a TikTok video: in TikTok, tap Share → Copy link. Full links (tiktok.com/@name/video/…) and short links (vm.tiktok.com/…) both work, and you can paste TikTok’s whole share text. See How to save a TikTok sound.

Why does it say “That’s a sound, hashtag or profile page”?

Those pages don’t point to one sound. Open a video that uses the sound you want and copy that video’s link instead.

Why did a lookup fail?

Tikify finds sounds through tikwm.com, a free third-party service. When it’s busy or down, wait a moment and try again. A private or deleted video can’t be looked up at all.

What if I save a sound I already have?

Tikify notices when a sound is already in your library and offers to use the saved one, so a sound used by many videos isn’t saved twice by accident.

Can I download TikTok sounds as MP3 files?

Tikify isn’t an MP3 downloader. It keeps your sounds inside Tikify, on your device, for listening in the app. A backup file holds your whole library, so you can restore it in Tikify on any device.

Listening

Do sounds play offline?

Yes. Saved sounds are downloaded to your device and play without internet. See Listen offline.

Does it keep playing with the screen locked?

Yes. Tikify keeps playing in the background and moves on to the next sound by itself. Use the lock screen, Control Center, headphones or car to play, pause and skip.

Can I shuffle and repeat?

Yes: shuffle any playlist, repeat a whole playlist or loop one sound. See Playlists.

Your library

Where are my sounds stored?

Only on your device, in your browser’s storage. There’s no Tikify server, so your library isn’t synced between devices.

How do I move my sounds to a new phone?

Open Your Library → ⋮ → Export backup on the old device, then Import backup on the new one.

I installed Tikify on my iPhone and my sounds are gone

They’re still in Safari. On iPhone the Home Screen app has its own storage. Open tikify.no in Safari, export a backup there, then import it in the Home Screen app.

Can my library get deleted?

A browser can clear a website’s storage when the device runs low on space or when you clear your browsing data, and Safari can clear it for websites you haven’t opened in a while. Tikify asks the browser to protect its storage, and installing it on your home screen helps too. To be safe, export a backup now and then.

About Tikify

Is Tikify made by TikTok?

No. Tikify is an independent project. It isn’t made, endorsed or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance.

What does Tikify do with my data?

There’s no account, no analytics and no Tikify server: your library stays on your device. See Privacy for exactly what is sent where.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Open an issue on GitHub.
