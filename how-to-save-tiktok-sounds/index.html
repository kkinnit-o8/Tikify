How to save a TikTok sound and listen to it later

Heard a sound on TikTok you want to hear again? With Tikify you save the sound itself, so you can play it like a song: in playlists, offline and with your phone locked. It takes about ten seconds.

The short version

  1. In TikTok, open a video that uses the sound and tap Share → Copy link.
  2. Open Tikify and tap Add sound.
  3. Paste the link, tap Find sound, then Save to library.

Step by step

  1. Find a video that uses the sound

    Any TikTok video you can open works. It has to be a video: a sound’s own page, a hashtag or a profile doesn’t point to one sound, so Tikify can’t read it. On a sound’s page, open one of the videos that use it.

  2. Copy the video’s link

    Tap Share (the arrow on the right of the video), then Copy link. Short links like vm.tiktok.com/… work, and so does TikTok’s whole share text: Tikify finds the link in it.

  3. Paste it into Tikify

    Open Tikify and tap Add sound at the bottom. Tap the paste button in the box (or long-press the box and choose Paste), then Find sound.

  4. Check the sound and give it a name

    Tikify shows the sound with its cover: tap play to hear it first. You can change the title and the artist. Sounds made on TikTok are often just called “original sound”, so Tikify offers the video’s caption as a title instead. Tick the playlists you want it in.

  5. Save it

    Tap Save to library. Tikify downloads the sound and its cover to your device, so from now on it plays without internet.

Even quicker on Android

Once Tikify is installed on your phone, you can skip copying and pasting: in TikTok, tap Share, then More and choose Tikify. The Add screen opens with the link already in it.

On iPhone, install Tikify first

If you’ll use Tikify from your Home Screen, add it there before you save sounds. On iPhone the Home Screen app has its own storage, separate from Safari, so sounds saved in Safari don’t show up in it. You can still move them over with Export backup and Import backup.

If it doesn’t work

“That’s a sound, hashtag or profile page”

The link doesn’t point to a single video. Open a video that uses the sound, tap Share → Copy link and paste that.

“Couldn’t find a video at that link”

The video may be private or deleted, or part of the link got lost when you copied it. Copy it again from TikTok’s Share menu.

“Too many lookups right now”

Tikify looks links up through tikwm.com, a free service that answers about one request a second. Wait a few seconds and try again.

“This TikTok has no sound that can be downloaded”

Some videos have their sound muted or removed. Try another video that uses the same sound.

Is it OK to save sounds?

Save sounds for your own listening, and respect the rights of the people who made them. Tikify keeps the sounds inside the app on your device. It doesn’t share or publish anything.
