Listen to TikTok sounds offline, in the background and with the screen locked

TikTok is made for watching: the sound stops when you switch apps or lock your phone, and nothing plays without internet. Tikify turns the sounds you save into a music library that keeps playing.

  • OfflineSaved sounds are stored on your device and play without internet.
  • In the backgroundKeep listening while you use other apps.
  • With the screen lockedTikify goes on from one sound to the next by itself.
  • Lock-screen controlsPlay, pause and skip from the lock screen, Control Center, headphones or car.

How it works

When you save a sound, Tikify downloads the audio and its cover and keeps them in your browser’s storage on your device. Playing a saved sound doesn’t use the internet at all, so it works on a plane, in the subway or with mobile data turned off. Tikify itself opens offline too, once you’ve opened it online one time.

Listen with the screen off

Start a sound or a playlist, then lock your phone or switch to another app. Tikify keeps playing and moves on to the next sound by itself. The lock screen shows what’s playing, with buttons for play, pause, previous and next, which skip whole sounds. Headphone and car controls work the same way.

Shuffle and repeat work in the background too: repeat a whole playlist, or loop one sound for as long as you like.

Get the most out of it

  • Install Tikify. Add it to your home screen: it opens full screen like an app, and browsers are less likely to clear an installed app’s storage.
  • On iPhone, install before you save sounds. The Home Screen app has its own storage, separate from Safari.
  • Make a backup now and then. Your library lives only on your device. In Tikify, open Your Library → ⋮ → Export backup to save it to a file, and Import backup to bring it back or move it to another phone.

Questions

Does Tikify use mobile data?

Only when you add a sound: it looks the link up and downloads the sound once. Playing saved sounds uses no data.

A sound says it needs internet. Why?

If the download fails while you save, Tikify can save the sound without downloading it. That sound plays only online at first, and Tikify downloads it the next time it plays with a connection.

How much space do sounds take?

TikTok sounds are short, so they take little room: usually around a megabyte each, often less. Tikify shows how much it uses under Your Library → ⋮.
